# Portfolio provenance

Use this guide to describe your work honestly.

## Contribution record

For each portfolio project or substantial submission, record:

- **Artifact:** file, feature, test suite, or report.
- **Your work:** what you personally analyzed, wrote, executed, or changed.
- **Assistance:** tools or AI assistance used and the parts they helped with.
- **Your understanding:** what you can explain, maintain, and reproduce independently.

## Example

> I independently designed and executed the exploratory test session and wrote the bug report. AI assistance helped scaffold the training application and review wording. I can explain the expected behavior, reproduce the defect, and discuss the evidence.

Do not claim generated code, tests, or documentation as independently authored. Do not include credentials, real user data, or confidential employer information in this public repository.
