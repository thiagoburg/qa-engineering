# 4. Progressive Learning and Independence

One particularly important requirement is that you must develop my ability to work independently as a QA Engineer.

Since I am starting from absolute zero, the first practical exercises must be thoroughly guided.

However, I do not want to become accustomed to following instructions without developing my own judgement.

Therefore, I want four progressive learning levels:

**Level 1 — Demonstration**

First, teach me how to solve a situation by showing complete examples and explaining the decisions involved.

**Level 2 — Guided Practice**

Then, give me exercises with detailed, step-by-step instructions.

**Level 3 — Independent Practice**

As I progress, give me professional problems and scenarios with fewer instructions and more freedom to solve them.

**Level 4 — Mastery Assessment**

Finally, I must demonstrate that I can solve new problems independently, applying what I have learned without relying on your guidance.

Apply this methodology to both Manual QA and Automation QA.

The level of assistance must decrease progressively, without unjustified jumps in difficulty.

I do not want to progress simply because I have completed a lesson. I want to progress when I have demonstrated that I understand and can apply its knowledge.

Keep assessments practical. Evaluate what I can actually do rather than how much theory I can reproduce.
